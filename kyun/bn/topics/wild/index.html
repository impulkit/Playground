But the ground can feel them.Can plants move?Touch a chhui-mui leaf and it folds shut in seconds. It's actually one tree.How did Kaziranga save the one-horned rhino?A hundred years ago there may have been only a few dozen rhinos in Kaziranga. In a good sanctuary, the animals mostly get left alone.What is the difference between turtles and tortoises?Every tortoise is a turtle, but not every turtle is a tortoise. One tortoise, Jonathan, has been plodding around an island garden since the 1830s.What is the lion-tailed macaque?A shy black monkey with a silver mane lives only in the rainforests of the Western Ghats, and by 2026 rope bridges had been strung across roads to stop it being run over.What is the toughest animal on Earth?An animal smaller than a full stop has survived the open vacuum of space, been frozen close to the coldest temperature possible, and come back to life.What kinds of deer live in India?In Manipur, a rare deer lives on islands of floating plants and steps so lightly that people call it the dancing deer.What wild animals live in cities?Leopards live inside the city limits of Mumbai, and a mountain lion once lived in the hills behind the Hollywood sign.Where do otters live in India?India has three kinds of otter, including the smallest otter on Earth, and most people living near them have never seen one.Where does chocolate come from?From seeds inside a pod that grows straight out of a tree trunk.Which animal is the strongest for its size?A dung beetle smaller than your fingernail can pull more than 1,000 times its own weight, like a child dragging a train of loaded trucks.Which animals are endangered today, and why?In the 1990s, India's vultures almost vanished in about ten years. The cause was hiding in a common painkiller.Which animals live the longest?Somewhere in the Arctic Ocean there may be sharks still swimming that were born before the Taj Mahal was finished.Which bears live in India?India is home to four kinds of bear, including one that sucks up termites like a vacuum cleaner and carries its cubs on its back.Which land animals migrate?Every year, more than a million wildebeest walk in a giant loop across Tanzania and Kenya, chasing the rain.Who pollinates our food?Every vanilla pod in the world owes something to a 12-year-old boy on an island in the Indian Ocean in 1841.Why are Asiatic lions found only in Gir?Lions once roamed from the Middle East to Bihar. Something doesn't add up.Why are wetlands important?In Kolkata, a marsh quietly cleans a big share of a giant city's dirty water, and grows fish while it does it.Why did cheetahs come back to India?They disappeared from India 70 years ago.
