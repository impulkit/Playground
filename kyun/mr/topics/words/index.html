Are emojis a language?In 2015, the Word of the Year chosen by Oxford Dictionaries wasn't a word at all. It was a yellow face crying with laughter.Can people talk by whistling?On a steep island in the Atlantic, children learn at school to whistle whole sentences across valleys too deep to shout over.Can someone invent a language?In 1887, an eye doctor in Warsaw published a small booklet containing a brand-new language. Today some children grow up speaking it.How are picture books made?A picture book may have fewer words than this page, yet it can take an author and an artist two years to finish.How did Amar Chitra Katha bring history to comics?It began because a man watching a TV quiz was upset that Indian children knew Greek gods better than their own country's stories.How did Dr. Seuss make reading fun?Green Eggs and Ham uses only 50 different words. Dr. Seuss wrote it to win a bet.How did English spread around the world?About 1,500 years ago, English was spoken by a few groups of farmers in what is now England. Today it is the shared language of pilots, scientists and the internet.How did Hindi grow?Hindi grew from the everyday speech of the country around Delhi. Today it is one of the most spoken languages on Earth.How did India tell stories before books?Some Indian priests can chant long Vedic hymns from memory, word for word, as they were passed down for about 3,000 years.How did Latin give us so many English words?English is a Germanic language, a cousin of German and Dutch, yet more than half the words in a big English dictionary come from Latin.How did Marathi and Gujarati grow?In 1960, a single Indian state split in two along a line drawn by language, and Maharashtra and Gujarat were born on the same day.How did Tinkle comics start?Suppandi, the boy who follows every instruction exactly and gets everything wrong, grew out of folk tales that readers posted to Tinkle.How did Urdu develop?Urdu was born in the markets and army camps of north India, where Persian, Arabic and Turkish words met the everyday speech of Delhi.How did libraries begin?One of the oldest libraries ever found was made of clay. In Rick Riordan's books, Mount Olympus floats above the Empire State Building, on the 600th floor.How do spelling bee champions spell?In 2026, a 14-year-old from California won the Scripps National Spelling Bee after spelling 32 words correctly in 90 seconds, a new record.How do translators work?In German, someone who doesn't notice the obvious "has tomatoes on their eyes". Translating that word for word would leave English readers baffled.How do you write a good story?Roald Dahl wrote almost all his books in a small hut at the bottom of his garden, sitting in an old armchair with a board across his knees.How does Braille let people read with fingers?A French teenager, blind since the age of five, invented a way to read with six raised dots. He was 15.How does Chinese writing work?Chinese has no alphabet. Instead, it has thousands of characters, and each one stands for a whole syllable with a meaning of its own.How does sign language work?There isn't one sign language. There are hundreds, and American and British Sign Language are so different that their users can't easily understand each other.How is a book made?The pages of most books are not printed one at a time. They are printed many to a giant sheet, which is then folded, sewn and sliced.How is a newspaper made?A morning newspaper is written, checked, designed, printed and delivered to your door in less time than you spend asleep.How is ink made?The purple mark painted on your finger when you vote in India is made from a chemical that reacts with your skin and won't wash off for days.How many languages does India speak?In the 2011 census, Indians gave more than 19,000 different names for their mother tongue.How old are Kannada and Telugu?Carved on stones more than 1,400 years ago, Kannada and Telugu still look like cousins today, with round, looping letters that seem to be smiling.How were encyclopedias made?In 1408, more than 2,000 scholars in China finished an encyclopedia of over 11,000 volumes. Fewer than 4 in 100 survive today.Is emoji a language?In 2015, the Oxford dictionaries picked a "word" of the year that was not a word at all. It was the crying-with-laughter face.What are Feluda and Professor Shonku?One of India's greatest film-makers also wrote dozens of stories about a sharp-eyed detective and a scientist who invented a pill that cures almost everything.What are secret languages?For centuries, women in one corner of China wrote to each other in a secret script that most men could not read.What are some African folk tales?In a story from Ghana, a clever spider named Anansi tricked a python, a leopard and a swarm of hornets to buy every story in the world from the sky god.What are some Japanese folk legends?In Japanese folklore, if you meet a river monster called a kappa, the smart move is to bow politely.What are some famous Chinese legends?An archer shot nine suns out of the sky. A monkey made of stone fought an army of heaven. And a monk walked all the way to India for books.What are some favourite Indian folk tales?A monkey who tricks a crocodile by claiming he left his heart up in a tree is a story told in India for well over 2,000 years, and versions turn up from Persia to France.What are some great adventure books?Treasure Island began as a map a writer drew to amuse his stepson on a rainy holiday in Scotland in 1881.What are some poems every child should know?Twinkle, Twinkle, Little Star was written in 1806 by Jane Taylor, a young woman in England, and children on every continent still sing it.What are the Buddhist scriptures?For about four hundred years, the Buddha's teachings were not written down at all. Monks carried them in their memories and chanted them aloud together.What are the Jain scriptures?For about a thousand years, Jain monks carried their holy teachings only in memory, until a great council in Gujarat finally wrote them down.What are the Jataka tales?Before he became the Buddha, Buddhists say, he lived hundreds of lives, as a monkey king, a deer, an elephant and a generous hare.What are the Norse myths?Four days of your week are named after gods worshipped by the Vikings, including a red-bearded thunder god with a magic hammer.What are the Vikram and Betal stories?A brave king carries a talking spirit through a dark forest. Every morning, it won.What did the Maya believe about the world?According to an ancient Maya story, the gods tried to make people three times. She was the last speaker of Bo, a language spoken there for thousands of years.What is Charlotte's Web about?A spider saves a pig's life by weaving words into her web. E. B. White based her on a real spider in his barn.What is Malgudi Days?Malgudi has a railway station, a river, a market road and a school full of mischief. It just doesn't exist on any map.What is Narnia?It began with a picture that popped into a 16-year-old's head, of a faun carrying an umbrella and parcels through a snowy wood.What is Sangam poetry?About 2,000 years ago, Tamil poets wrote a line still quoted today: "Every town is our town, everyone our kin."What is Sanskrit?About 2,400 years ago, a scholar named Panini described the whole of Sanskrit grammar in about 4,000 short rules. Linguists still study his work.What is a graphic novel?In 1992, a comic book about mice and cats won a special Pulitzer Prize. It told a true story of the Holocaust.What is a haiku?The most famous haiku in the world is about a frog jumping into a pond. That's it, and people have been talking about it for more than 300 years.What is a palindrome?The name of a language spoken by more than 3 crore people in Kerala reads the same backwards as forwards. Try spelling Malayalam backwards.What is science fiction?In 1905, a Bengali woman wrote a story about a land where women run everything, cook with solar power and fly in air-cars. It is still read as science fiction today.What is the Arabian Nights?The most famous story in the Arabian Nights, Aladdin, was never in the old Arabic books at all.What is the Bhagavad Gita?The Bhagavad Gita begins with a warrior so upset before a battle that he drops his bow and refuses to fight.What is the Bible?The Bible isn't really one book. Its name comes from a Greek word meaning "the books", and inside are dozens of them, written over many centuries.What is the Booker Prize?Every autumn, writers across the world wait for one envelope to be opened in London. Winning can turn a quiet novel into a worldwide bestseller.What is the Gurmukhi script?The same Punjabi words can be written in two completely different scripts, depending on which side of the India-Pakistan border you live on.What is the Guru Granth Sahib?The holy book of the Sikhs is treated as a living Guru, and it includes poems by Hindu and Muslim saints alongside those of the Sikh Gurus.What is the Hitopadesha?Hundreds of years ago, a teacher was given six months to turn some lazy princes into wise ones. His secret weapon was a book of animal stories.What is the Mahabharata?It has about 100,000 verses, making it one of the longest poems ever composed. Hidden inside it is the Bhagavad Gita.What is the Nobel Prize in Literature?Leo Tolstoy, author of War and Peace, never won it. Bob Dylan, a singer, did.What is the Quran?Millions of people around the world, many of them children, have learned the whole Quran by heart, all 114 chapters of it, in Arabic.What is the Ramayana?A prince, a princess, a demon king and an army of monkeys. The Ramayana has been retold for more than 2,000 years, from Indian villages to Thai palaces.What is the Thirukkural?An ancient Tamil book packs advice on almost everything in life into 1,330 tiny poems of just seven words each.What is the longest word in English?The longest word in major English dictionaries has 45 letters, and it was invented in 1935 by a puzzle fan as a joke.What is the story behind The Jungle Book?The most famous story about an Indian jungle was written in a snowy farmhouse in Vermont, USA, by a man born in Bombay.What is unique about Malayalam?Spell "Malayalam" backwards and you get Malayalam again. It is one of the very few language names that is a palindrome.What magazines did Indian children read?For decades, Indian children waited each month for a magazine where a stubborn king carried a talking ghost on his back.What makes Bengali special?In 1952, students in Dhaka died fighting for the right to use Bengali. The whole world now marks that day as Mother Language Day.What makes Ruskin Bond's stories special?He wrote his first novel at seventeen and has spent more than sixty years writing about the same Himalayan hills from the same small window.What makes a speech famous?Abraham Lincoln's most famous speech took about two minutes. The man who spoke before him talked for two hours, and almost nobody remembers his words.What makes fantasy books so popular?J. R. R. Tolkien invented whole languages first, then wrote The Lord of the Rings partly to give them a world to live in.What makes something a poem?The oldest writer in history whose name we know was a poet, a priestess in Mesopotamia about 4,300 years ago.What stories do Native Americans tell?In one Native American story, the whole of North America sits on the back of a giant turtle. Many Native people still call the continent Turtle Island.What was Chandamama magazine?For decades, every month, a king in Chandamama carried a corpse through a dark forest while a ghost on his shoulder told riddles.What was the Bengal Renaissance?For about a hundred years, one city produced reformers, poets, scientists and a Nobel Prize winner, and changed how Indians thought about themselves.Where did mermaid stories come from?In January 1493, Christopher Columbus wrote that he saw three mermaids rise from the sea, and that they were "not as pretty as they are painted".Where did the alphabet come from?The letter A began as a picture of an ox's head. Turn a capital A upside down and you can still see the horns.Where did the idea of unicorns come from?Around 400 BCE, a Greek doctor wrote that India was home to wild horse-like beasts with a single magic horn. He had never been to India.Where do idioms come from?When a cricket fan says a piece of news "hit me for six", they are using a phrase that began on a cricket ground.Where does slang come from?OK began in 1839 as a jokey newspaper abbreviation. It is now one of the most widely understood words in the world.Which English words came from India?Every time you put on pyjamas, wash with shampoo or talk about a jungle, you are borrowing from Indian languages.Which is the hardest language to learn?The US government's language school estimates that an English speaker needs about 88 weeks of full-time classes to get good at Japanese, but only about 24 weeks for Spanish.Who are some famous Indian writers in English?English arrived in India with traders and rulers. Indian writers took it, bent it, filled it with chai and monsoon, and made it their own.Who are the world's most famous detectives?Sherlock Holmes never once says "Elementary, my dear Watson" in any of the 60 stories Arthur Conan Doyle wrote about him.Who decides what goes in a dictionary?Dictionaries don't invent words or decide which ones are allowed. They watch how millions of people actually talk and write.Who invented paper?The first people to make paper weren't humans. Wasps have been chewing wood into paper nests for millions of years.Who invented punctuation?For centuries, Greek and Roman books had no spaces, no full stops and no commas, just one long river of capital letters.Who is Garuda?Garuda, the giant bird who carries the god Vishnu, is also the national symbol of Indonesia and Thailand, and the name of an Indian Air Force commando unit.Who is Sherlock Holmes?In 1893 Arthur Conan Doyle killed off Sherlock Holmes at a Swiss waterfall. Readers were so upset that, ten years later, he brought him back.Who is Sudha Murty?In 1974 a young engineer named Sudha Kulkarni sent an angry postcard to the head of India's biggest company because a job advert said women need not apply, and it worked.Who makes up new words?Dr Seuss gave us the earliest known use of "nerd", and a dictionary once printed a word that didn't exist for 13 years.Who was Aesop?The man behind the tortoise, the hare and the boy who cried wolf may have been an enslaved storyteller, and he may never have existed.Who was Charles Dickens?At twelve, Charles Dickens was sticking labels on pots of boot polish in a rat-infested factory. He grew up to become the most famous writer of his age.Who was J.R.R. Tolkien?Marking dull exam papers, a professor found a blank page and wrote: "In a hole in the ground there lived a hobbit."Who was Jules Verne?Jules Verne's Captain Nemo, the mysterious commander of the submarine Nautilus, turns out to be an Indian prince who fought the British in 1857.Who was Kalidasa?India's most famous Sanskrit poet once wrote a whole poem in which a cloud carries a love letter across the country.Who was Langston Hughes?At 19, on a train crossing the Mississippi, he scribbled a poem on the back of an envelope. Americans still learn it by heart.Who was Mark Twain?He was born when Halley's Comet was in the sky, predicted he would leave with it, and died the day after it came back.Who was Maya Angelou?As a girl she stopped speaking for about five years. She grew up to read a poem to millions at a US president's swearing-in.Who was Munshi Premchand?His most loved story is about a small boy who spends his only coins at the Eid fair on a pair of iron tongs for his grandmother.Who was R.K. He never forgot it.Why are fairy tales so old?Some scientists think a few fairy tales are older than writing, older even than the wheel.Why are puns funny?About 900 years ago, an Indian poet wrote a single poem that tells the Ramayana and the Mahabharata at the same time, using puns in every verse.Why are there dragons in stories all over the world?For centuries, Chinese medicine shops sold "dragon bones". It's your brain, planning the next sounds before you've finished the last ones.Why did writers from Britain write about India?George Orwell, author of Animal Farm, was born in a small town in Bihar. Many British writers' lives began or changed in India.Why do Panchatantra stories use animals?A clever hare tricks a lion into jumping down a well. Stories like this were first told to teach princes how to rule.Why do animals say different things in different languages?An Indian dog says "bhau bhau", a Japanese dog says "wan wan" and an American dog says "woof", yet all three bark exactly alike.Why do languages have grammar?"Dog bites man" is a boring headline. "Man bites dog" is big news. Same words, different order: that's grammar at work.Why do names have meanings?Almost every name was once an ordinary word. Aditya means sun, Smith meant a metal worker, and Johnson meant "son of John".Why do people from the same country speak differently?An old Hindi saying goes "kos kos par badle paani, chaar kos par baani".
