What is the NBA?

The game began with two peach baskets nailed to a balcony. Today its best league plays in front of fans from Toronto to Tokyo.

The short answer

The NBA, or National Basketball Association, is the top professional basketball league in the world. It has 30 teams, 29 in the USA and one, the Toronto Raptors, in Canada. Each team plays 82 games in the regular season, from October to April. The best teams then play knockout rounds, ending in the NBA Finals in June.

The NBA is famous for its stars, its fast, high-scoring games and its global reach. Its games are shown in more than 200 countries, and players from Europe, Africa and Asia now star in it.

Surprising but true

Wilt Chamberlain once scored 100 points in a single NBA game, in 1962. No one has come close to matching it since.

From peach baskets to a league

Basketball was invented in December 1891 by James Naismith, a teacher in Springfield, Massachusetts. He needed an indoor game for students during the winter. He nailed two peach baskets to a balcony and wrote 13 rules. Someone had to climb up to get the ball out after each basket.

The league that became the NBA started in 1946 as the Basketball Association of America. In 1949 it joined with a rival league and took the name NBA. Early teams played in small arenas and some went bust within a few seasons. Black players were first allowed in 1950, when Earl Lloyd, Chuck Cooper and Nat Clifton joined NBA teams.

Faster and higher

Early NBA games could be slow. In 1954 the league added the 24-second shot clock: a team must shoot within 24 seconds of getting the ball. Scoring went up straight away.

In 1979 the NBA added the three-point line. At first teams rarely used it. Today it is central to the game, and some teams take around 40 three-point shots a night. That is partly thanks to Stephen Curry of the Golden State Warriors, who has made more three-pointers than anyone in history.

Legends and records

Some numbers in the NBA seem impossible. On 2 March 1962 Wilt Chamberlain scored 100 points in one game. The Boston Celtics won 11 titles in 13 seasons between 1957 and 1969, led by Bill Russell. Michael Jordan won six titles with the Chicago Bulls in the 1990s and has the highest career scoring average.

In 2023 LeBron James passed Kareem Abdul-Jabbar to become the league's all-time top scorer. In 2024 he became the first player to reach 40,000 points.

How tall are they?

The basket is 3.05 metres high, the same as in Naismith's day. Most NBA players are very tall, often around 2 metres. Some are taller than 2.2 metres. Height helps with reaching the basket and blocking shots. Victor Wembanyama of France, who joined the league in 2023, is about 2.2 metres tall and can still dribble like a much smaller player.

But speed, skill and vision matter too. Muggsy Bogues was about 1.60 metres tall and played 14 seasons. Many of the best passers have been some of the shorter players on the court.

The NBA and India

Basketball is growing in India, especially in schools and cities. In 2015 the Dallas Mavericks drafted Satnam Singh Bhamara, the first player born and raised in India to be picked in the NBA draft. That same year, Sim Bhullar, a Canadian of Indian descent, became the first player of Indian heritage to play in an NBA game.

The NBA has run training programmes and an academy in India, and has played pre-season games in Mumbai. Many Indian children now know NBA players by name, wear their sneakers and practise their moves on local courts.

Myth or fact?

Myth: You have to be extremely tall to play in the NBA.

Truth: Most players are tall, but some stars have been fairly short. Muggsy Bogues, at about 1.60 metres, played 14 seasons in the NBA.

Try this

Play a game of "paper basketball" by throwing scrunched-up paper into a bin. Set a 24-second timer on a phone for each turn, so you must shoot before time runs out. Then try with 10 seconds and see how your aim changes under pressure.
