How does American football work?

An American football game has 60 minutes of playing time but usually takes about three hours, because the clock keeps stopping.

The short answer

American football is a game of two teams of 11 players on a long field. One team attacks, called the offense, and tries to carry or throw an oval ball into the other team's end zone. The other team, the defense, tries to stop them by tackling whoever has the ball.

The attackers get four tries, called downs, to move the ball forward 10 yards. If they manage it, they get four more tries. If they fail, the other team takes over. Touchdowns score six points. Kicking the ball between the tall goalposts scores three.

Surprising but true

A football is nicknamed the pigskin, but it is made of cowhide leather.

A game of short bursts

Unlike soccer, American football stops and starts. Each play, called a down, lasts only a few seconds. The teams line up facing each other at the line of scrimmage, the ball is passed back to the quarterback, and then everyone explodes into action.

The quarterback might hand the ball to a runner, or throw it far downfield to a receiver. When the ball carrier is tackled or runs out of bounds, the play ends. The teams huddle, plan the next move and line up again. The playing time is four 15-minute quarters, but stoppages mean a game usually lasts around three hours.

Scoring

A touchdown is worth 6 points. Afterwards the team can try an easy kick for 1 extra point, or run or pass into the end zone again for 2. A field goal, kicked through the posts from anywhere on the field, is worth 3. If the defense tackles an attacker in his own end zone, it scores a safety, worth 2.

It is a bit like an over in cricket. Each down is one delivery, and the team must get enough out of its four before the chance passes.

Born from rugby

The game grew out of rugby and soccer, which were both played at American colleges in the 1800s. The first college game, between Rutgers and Princeton in 1869, looked more like soccer. A Yale player and coach called Walter Camp then changed the rules in the 1880s, adding the line of scrimmage and the system of downs. He is often called the father of American football.

Early games were brutal, and players died. In 1905 President Theodore Roosevelt called college leaders to the White House to make the game safer. The forward pass was allowed the next year, which spread players out and changed the game for ever.

The NFL and the Super Bowl

The National Football League was founded in 1920 in Canton, Ohio. Its championship game, the Super Bowl, was first played in January 1967. It is now usually the most watched television broadcast in the United States each year, and companies pay millions for a 30-second advert.

Many Americans care just as much about high school and college football. In Texas, some high school stadiums hold more than 15,000 fans on a Friday night.

Helmets and heads

American football is a collision sport, and players wear helmets and pads. Doctors have found that repeated blows to the head can cause long-term brain damage, even without a serious single injury. Since about 2010 the NFL has changed rules to reduce head-to-head hits and protect players.

Sports scientists are designing better helmets, and many youth leagues now play flag football. Instead of tackling, players pull a flag from the ball carrier's belt. It keeps the plays and the strategy while dropping the hardest hits, a little like a game of kabaddi without the wrestling.

Myth or fact?

Myth: Footballs are made from pig skin.

Truth: Very early balls used an inflated pig's bladder inside a leather case, which is where the nickname comes from. Modern NFL balls are made of cowhide.

Try this

Draw a football field on a sheet of paper with lines every 10 yards. With a friend, take turns rolling a die four times; each roll moves your coin forward that many yards. If your four rolls add up to 10 or more, you get four new rolls. See who reaches the end zone first.
