What is the Asian Champions Trophy?

Every year or two, Asia's best hockey teams meet to decide who rules the continent. India has won it more often than anyone.

The short answer

The Asian Champions Trophy is a men's hockey tournament for the best teams in Asia. It is run by the Asian Hockey Federation and was first played in 2011.

Usually six teams take part, such as India, Pakistan, Japan, South Korea, Malaysia and China. They all play each other, then the top teams go through to the semi-finals and final. India has won it six times, more than any other team.

Surprising but true

India has no official national game. When someone asked the government in 2012, it said it had never declared one, not even hockey.

Why it matters

The Asian Champions Trophy sits between friendly matches and the giant tournaments. It is not a qualifier for the Olympics, but it lets Asia's top sides test new players and tactics against strong rivals.

Hosts take turns. It has been played in China, Japan, Bangladesh, Oman, Malaysia and India. In 2026, India won it in Mohali, Punjab, at a stadium named after Balbir Singh Senior, who won three Olympic golds.

How a comeback works

A hockey match has four quarters of 15 minutes each. Being a goal down early is not a disaster, because there is plenty of time left. In the 2026 final, Japan scored in the third minute, but India hit back with four goals in the second half.

Once a team equalises, it has momentum. Players grow confident, the crowd gets loud, and the other team starts to worry. Penalty corners help too. They are set moves teams practise for hours, often ending in a drag flick, a powerful scooping shot.

How is hockey played?In field hockey you may only hit the ball with the flat side of your stick, and a goal counts only if the shot comes from inside a marked semicircle.

A country that loves hockey

Indian hockey has a proud history. India won eight Olympic gold medals, six of them in a row from 1928 to 1956, with stars like Dhyan Chand. In recent years, India won Olympic bronze at Tokyo and Paris.

Many of India's players come from Punjab, Odisha and Jharkhand, where hockey is played in villages, schools and clubs.

Myth or fact?

Myth: Hockey is India's official national game.

Truth: India has never officially named a national game. Hockey is loved because of India's great Olympic history, but no law or order makes it the national sport.

Try this

Roll a ball along the floor and try to stop it with a ruler held flat, then with the ruler tilted. Notice how the angle changes where the ball goes. Hockey players use the same idea when they deflect the ball towards goal.
