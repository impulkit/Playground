What is Studio Ghibli?

Studio Ghibli is named after a hot desert wind, because its founders wanted to blow a fresh wind through Japanese animation.

The short answer

Studio Ghibli is a Japanese animation studio in Tokyo, famous for hand-drawn films full of forests, flying machines, spirits and brave young heroes. It was founded in 1985 by the directors Hayao Miyazaki and Isao Takahata, and the producer Toshio Suzuki.

Its best-known films include My Neighbour Totoro, Kiki's Delivery Service, Princess Mononoke and Spirited Away. They're loved by children and adults around the world, partly because they take children's feelings seriously and rarely have simple villains. Two Ghibli films have won the Oscar for Best Animated Feature.

Surprising but true

Spirited Away (2001) was the highest-earning film ever shown in Japan for almost twenty years, beating every Hollywood blockbuster.

A wind from the desert

The name Ghibli comes from an Italian word for a hot wind that blows off the Sahara. It was also the name of an Italian aeroplane, and Miyazaki has loved aircraft since childhood, when his father's family business made aircraft parts. The founders hoped to blow a new wind through Japanese animation.

Why does the wind blow?Every breeze you feel began with the Sun heating some places more than others.

The studio's logo is Totoro, a huge, furry forest spirit from My Neighbour Totoro (1988). In the film, two sisters move to the countryside and discover him in the woods. In its best-known scene, they wait beside him at a rainy bus stop.

Drawn by hand

Most big animated films today are made with 3D computer animation. Ghibli is famous for sticking mainly with hand-drawn pictures, painted backgrounds and careful attention to small things: wind in the grass, steam rising from a bowl, a girl tying her shoe.

Computers do help with colouring and some effects, but the main drawings are done by people. A Ghibli film can take years to make, with tens of thousands of drawings.

Big themes, quiet moments

Ghibli films often deal with serious ideas: war, growing up, the loss of forests and the balance between people and nature. Princess Mononoke (1997) shows a fight between forest gods and a town that cuts down trees to make iron.

They also have long, calm scenes where nothing much happens, like watching rain or waiting for a train. Miyazaki has said these pauses give viewers room to breathe and feel.

Many of the heroes are girls, such as Kiki the young witch who sets up a flying delivery service, and Chihiro in Spirited Away. They are brave, but they also feel scared and make mistakes.

Spirited Away and the Oscars

Spirited Away (2001) follows a ten-year-old girl trapped in a bathhouse for spirits. It became the highest-earning film ever in Japan, a record it held until 2020. It won the Oscar for Best Animated Feature in 2003.

In 2024, Miyazaki's The Boy and the Heron won the same award. He was in his eighties, and had announced his retirement several times before coming back to make it.

Ghibli in a new age

Fans can visit the Ghibli Museum in Mitaka, Tokyo, which opened in 2001, and Ghibli Park in Aichi, which opened in 2022.

In 2025, millions of people used AI image tools to turn their photos into pictures in a "Ghibli style". It started a debate. Some found it harmless fun. Others, including many artists, argued that it copied a style built over decades of human work, without permission. Years earlier, Miyazaki had reacted with strong dislike to an AI animation demo, and his words spread widely again. The argument about who owns an artistic style is still going on.

Myth or fact?

Myth: My Neighbour Totoro has a hidden dark meaning, and Totoro is really a spirit of death.

Truth: This is an internet rumour. Studio Ghibli publicly denied it in 2007, saying the film has no such hidden story. Totoro is simply a forest spirit.

Try this

Ghibli films often show food cooked slowly and lovingly. Help an adult make a simple snack, then try drawing it the way an animator might, paying attention to steam, shine and colour. Notice how much detail it takes to make food look tasty on paper.
