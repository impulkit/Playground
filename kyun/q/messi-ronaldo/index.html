Why are Messi and Ronaldo so famous?

For about fifteen years, two players won almost every big individual award in football between them. Their fans have argued about who is better ever since.

The short answer

Lionel Messi of Argentina and Cristiano Ronaldo of Portugal are two of the greatest footballers ever. From 2008 to 2023 they won the Ballon d'Or, the most famous award for the world's best player, 13 times between them. Both scored hundreds of goals for their clubs and countries and broke record after record.

They are very different. Messi is small and quiet, with close dribbling and clever passes. Ronaldo is tall and powerful, a great header of the ball and a fierce athlete. Fans love to argue about which one is better, and the debate shows no sign of ending.

Surprising but true

As a boy, Lionel Messi was found to have a growth hormone problem. FC Barcelona agreed to help pay for his treatment when he moved to Spain at 13.

Messi: the small boy from Rosario

Messi was born in Rosario, Argentina, in 1987. He was obviously gifted, but at about 10 doctors found he did not produce enough growth hormone. The treatment was expensive. FC Barcelona offered to help, and at 13 he moved to Spain with his family.

He grew to about 1.70 metres. At Barcelona he became the club's all-time top scorer, and won the Champions League four times. His low centre of gravity let him change direction almost instantly, which made him very hard to tackle.

Ronaldo: the boy from an island

Cristiano Ronaldo was born in 1985 in Funchal, on the Portuguese island of Madeira. He left home at 12 to join Sporting Lisbon's academy. At 18 he moved to Manchester United in England.

Ronaldo is famous for his training. He works on strength, speed and jumping, and keeps a strict diet. He has won the Champions League five times, with Manchester United and Real Madrid, and is its all-time top scorer. He is also the top scorer in men's international football, with more than 130 goals for Portugal by 2024.

Rivals in Spain

From 2009 to 2018 Ronaldo played for Real Madrid while Messi played for Barcelona. Their matches, called El Clásico, became some of the most-watched club games in the world. Each season one of them seemed to break a scoring record set by the other.

Messi won the Ballon d'Or a record eight times, the last in 2023. Ronaldo won it five times. Many experts say having such a rival pushed both to be better. In the 2011–12 season Messi scored 73 goals for Barcelona in all competitions, and in 2012 he scored 91 goals in a calendar year, a world record at the time.

Trophies for their countries

For years, critics said Messi could not win for Argentina. He lost a World Cup final in 2014 and several Copa América finals. Then he won the Copa América in 2021 and the World Cup in Qatar in 2022, scoring twice in a thrilling final against France.

Ronaldo led Portugal to their first major trophy at Euro 2016, even though he was injured early in the final and cheered from the side. He became the first man to score at five World Cups.

Beyond football

Both are hugely popular online. Ronaldo has more Instagram followers than any other person, and Messi is not far behind. Their social media posts reach more people than many TV channels.

In 2023 Messi joined Inter Miami in the USA, and Ronaldo moved to Al Nassr in Saudi Arabia. Indian fans have their own memories: Messi played for Argentina at Kolkata's Salt Lake Stadium in 2011, in a friendly against Venezuela, and a huge crowd came to watch.

Myth or fact?

Myth: Lionel Messi never won a big trophy for Argentina.

Truth: That was said for years, but it is no longer true. Messi won the Copa América in 2021 and 2024 and the World Cup in 2022.
