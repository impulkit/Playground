What is a thali?

A thali puts a whole meal on one plate, and every region of India fills it differently.

The short answer

Thali simply means plate. But in Indian food, a thali is also a complete meal served on one large plate, usually made of steel, brass or a banana leaf. Small bowls called katoris sit around the edge, filled with dal, vegetables, curd and more. Rice or rotis go in the middle, with pickle, papad and a sweet on the side.

The idea is balance. A thali gives you a bit of everything: grains, pulses, vegetables and dairy, and many different tastes in one sitting. Every region of India has its own version, so a thali in Gujarat looks very different from one in Bengal.

Surprising but true

India's Economic Survey of 2019–20 had a whole chapter called "Thalinomics", measuring how affordable a plate of food was for ordinary workers.

A meal on one plate

A typical north Indian thali might hold rotis, rice, dal, two vegetable dishes, paneer, raita, pickle, salad and a small sweet like gulab jamun. Eating it is a little like a journey: you mix a bite of dal with rice, then a bite of sabzi with roti, then a spoon of curd.

Many restaurants serve "unlimited thalis", where servers keep refilling the bowls until you say stop. In Gujarati and Rajasthani restaurants, the server often comes round with buckets, ladling more dal before you have asked.

Six tastes on a plate

Ayurveda, India's traditional system of medicine, describes six tastes: sweet, sour, salty, pungent, bitter and astringent. Ayurvedic texts suggest that a good meal includes all six for balance.

Whether or not people follow Ayurveda, many thalis do cover all six. There is sweet kheer, sour pickle or kadhi, salty papad, pungent chutney, bitter methi or karela, and astringent foods like pomegranate or some leafy greens.

A thali for every state

India's regions each fill the plate differently. A Gujarati thali has dal and kadhi that are gently sweet, along with dhokla and farsan snacks. A Rajasthani thali might feature dal baati churma and ker sangri, a dish of desert berries and beans.

In Bengal, a meal may come in courses, starting with bitter shukto and ending with mishti doi. Maharashtra has varan bhaat and puran poli. In Punjab, you might find sarson ka saag with makki ki roti.

A banana-leaf feast

In south India, meals are often served on a banana leaf. In Kerala, the Onam sadya is a famous feast with over 20 dishes, from sambar and avial to payasam. Each dish has its own place on the leaf, and the leaf is laid with its tip pointing to the guest's left.

What are Onam and Pongal?On Pongal morning in Tamil Nadu, families watch a pot of rice and milk on the fire, waiting for it to boil over. When it does, everyone shouts "Pongalo Pongal!"

Banana leaves are clean, waterproof and leave nothing behind when thrown away. Some people also say food tastes better on them.

Thalinomics

In 2020, India's Economic Survey, a yearly report by the Ministry of Finance, included a chapter called "Thalinomics". It studied how much a simple vegetarian or non-vegetarian thali cost compared with what a worker earned. The survey found that a thali had become more affordable for workers over the previous decade or so.

The thali abroad

Indian restaurants in the United States often serve thalis, especially at lunch. Many Americans meet the idea there for the first time. A home-cooked rice and dal meal, packed into a tiffin, is also what Mumbai's dabbawalas carry across the city every day. Wherever it is served, a thali is a small map of the kitchen it came from.

Myth or fact?

Myth: Indian food is always very spicy.

Truth: Many Indian dishes are mild. Curd rice, plain dal, khichdi, many sweets and Gujarati dishes are gentle and often a little sweet. Chilli heat varies hugely from region to region and from family to family.

Try this

Draw a big circle for a plate and plan your own thali. Try to include all six Ayurvedic tastes, such as sweet kheer, sour lemon pickle, salty papad, pungent chutney, bitter karela and astringent pomegranate. Ask your family which tastes your meals usually leave out.
