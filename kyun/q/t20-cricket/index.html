How did T20 cricket begin?

A Test match can last five days. A T20 match is over in about three hours, roughly the length of a film. That was exactly the idea.

The short answer

T20 is a short form of cricket in which each side bats for just 20 overs. It began in England in 2003, to bring crowds back to county cricket by fitting a whole match into an evening.

It caught on fast. The first T20 World Cup was held in 2007, India won it, and a year later the Indian Premier League began. Today T20 is the most-watched form of cricket on Earth.

Surprising but true

T20 was invented to bring crowds back to English county cricket, which few people had time to watch on working days. The first professional matches were played on summer evenings in 2003.

A game for busy people

For more than a century, top cricket meant Test matches, lasting up to five days, and later one-day matches of 50 overs each. Both took most of a working day or more. In England, crowds at county matches had shrunk.

So the England and Wales Cricket Board looked for a shorter game. A marketing manager, Stuart Robertson, asked fans what they wanted, and the answer was a match that took about three hours, after work or school. The first professional Twenty20 matches were played in June 2003, with music, fireworks and families in the stands.

How does cricket actually work?A cricket pitch is 22 yards long, about the length of two yellow school buses parked end to end, and nearly every rule starts there.

Special rules

A T20 match follows the same basic rules as other cricket, with some twists. In the first six overs, called the powerplay, only two fielders may stand outside the inner circle, so batters attack. A no-ball gives the batter a free hit, off which they cannot be bowled or caught out. Each bowler may bowl only four overs.

Because there are so few balls, every run counts. Strike rate, the runs scored per 100 balls, matters as much as a batter's average. Strong, light bats and big hitters have pushed scores higher and higher.

From county fields to the world

The first men's T20 international was played in Auckland, New Zealand, in 2005. In 2007, India, led by MS Dhoni, won the first T20 World Cup in South Africa. India's men won it again in 2024 and 2026.

The IPL, started in 2008, turned T20 into a giant show with players from many countries. T20 also helped cricket enter the Olympics: it returns at the 2028 Games in Los Angeles in this format. The short game's momentum keeps growing, with new records set almost every season and a steady stream of young tournament stars.

Myth or fact?

Myth: T20 is just slogging, with no skill.

Truth: Top T20 players plan every ball. Bowlers use slower balls, yorkers and wide lines, and batters study angles and fielders. Teams use data on every opponent, a lot like other professional sports.
