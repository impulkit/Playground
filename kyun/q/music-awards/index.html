What are the Grammys?

In 2024 tabla maestro Zakir Hussain won three Grammys in a single night, including one with his fusion band Shakti.

The short answer

The Grammy Awards, usually called the Grammys, are among the world's best-known music prizes. They're given each year in the United States by the Recording Academy, an organisation of people who make music, such as singers, songwriters, producers and sound engineers.

There are more than 90 categories, covering pop, rock, rap, jazz, classical, country, music for children and "global music" from around the world. The winners receive a small gold gramophone trophy. The first ceremony was held in 1959, and the show is now a big television event every February.

Surprising but true

The Grammy trophy is a tiny gold gramophone, an old record player with a horn. It's named after the gramophone, invented in the 1880s.

How winners are chosen

Record companies and artists first submit music released during the past year. Members of the Recording Academy then vote to choose the nominees, usually five to eight in each category. Members vote again to pick the winner. Accountants count the ballots in secret.

Members are meant to vote only in areas they know well, so a jazz drummer might vote in jazz categories but not in classical. Many people assume sales decide the result. They don't. A huge hit can lose to a quieter album that musicians admire, unlike music charts, which are based on sales and streams.

The Big Four

Four awards are open to every kind of music and are considered the biggest. Album of the Year goes to a whole album. Record of the Year goes to one recorded song, including how it was performed and produced. Song of the Year goes to the songwriters. Best New Artist goes to a newcomer.

Taylor Swift became the first artist to win Album of the Year four times, in 2024. Beyoncé has won more Grammys than anyone else, 35 by the 2025 ceremony, when she also won Album of the Year for the first time.

Who was Ravi Shankar?He played sitar at Woodstock, taught a Beatle, and helped organise one of the first giant charity rock concerts. But he always called himself a classical musician.

India at the Grammys

Ravi Shankar, the sitar master, won his first Grammy in 1967 for West Meets East, an album he made with the violinist Yehudi Menuhin. His daughter Anoushka Shankar has been nominated many times.

In 2010 A.R. Rahman won two Grammys for the music of Slumdog Millionaire. Bengaluru composer Ricky Kej has won several, the first in 2015. And at the 2024 ceremony, Zakir Hussain, the great tabla player, won three Grammys in one night. One was for This Moment, an album by the fusion band Shakti, whose members include Hussain, singer Shankar Mahadevan and guitarist John McLaughlin.

Why a gramophone

The trophy is shaped like a gramophone, an early machine for playing records. It had a wide horn to make the sound louder. The name Grammy comes from the same word. In the early days of sound recording, the gramophone helped turn music into something people could buy and play at home.

Each trophy is made by hand in a workshop in Colorado. It takes many hours to cast, polish and plate in gold. Winners don't usually get their own engraved trophy on the night. They hold a stand-in on stage, and the finished one arrives weeks later with their name on it.

Other music awards

The Grammys aren't the only music prizes. Britain has the Brit Awards. India's Filmfare Awards, started in 1954, include prizes for film music and playback singing, and the National Film Awards honour the best composers and singers every year.

The Grammys also face criticism. Some artists have said that Black musicians, women and non-English singers have too often been overlooked in the top categories. The Academy has added new members and categories, including one for African music performance in 2024. Plenty of musicians still say the real prize is a song people keep singing years later.

Myth or fact?

Myth: The Grammys go to whichever songs sold or streamed the most.

Truth: Winners are chosen by voting members of the Recording Academy, who are music professionals. Best-selling albums sometimes win, but often they don't, and smaller artists can beat big stars.

Try this

Make your own family music awards. Each person nominates two songs in categories like "best for dancing" and "best for a car ride". Play a short bit of each, vote secretly on slips of paper and hand the winner a gramophone drawn on card.
