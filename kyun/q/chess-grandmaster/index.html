How do you become a chess Grandmaster?

In 1988 India had one chess Grandmaster. In 2026, it got its hundredth. Each of them had to clear the same tough tests.

The short answer

A Grandmaster is the highest title in chess, apart from World Champion. It is given for life by FIDE, the world chess federation.

To earn it, a player usually needs two things: a rating of at least 2500, and three "norms", which are outstanding results in tournaments against strong players from several countries.

Surprising but true

India's 96th, 97th, 98th, 99th and 100th Grandmasters all earned their titles in just five months, between July and November 2026.

Ratings and norms

FIDE gives every serious player a rating, a number that goes up when you beat strong players and down when you lose. Beginners might be rated around 1000. A rating of 2500 puts you among the best few thousand players on Earth.

A norm is harder. You must score very well over at least nine games in a tournament where many opponents are titled players, including Grandmasters, from different countries. The maths behind ratings is a kind of probability: beating someone much stronger than you counts for a lot. Winning certain big championships can also count as a norm, or even give the title straight away.

A short history of the title

FIDE first gave out the Grandmaster title in 1950, to 27 players. Today there are about 2,000 in the whole world. There are other titles below it too, such as International Master and FIDE Master, and women's titles like Woman Grandmaster.

Chess itself grew from chaturanga, an Indian game played more than 1,400 years ago, where the pieces were based on an army. Yet modern India waited a long time for its first Grandmaster.

From one to a hundred

Viswanathan Anand earned the title in 1988, at 18, and went on to become World Champion five times. Children across the country saw that an Indian could reach the top.

Since then, chess academies have opened in many cities, especially in Tamil Nadu. Online games let a child in a small town play strong opponents every day, and computers that use a clever algorithm to check every move help players learn from mistakes. India reached 100 Grandmasters in November 2026, and young Indians such as Gukesh D, who became World Champion in 2024, are among the best in the world.

Myth or fact?

Myth: You have to be a genius born with a special brain to become a Grandmaster.

Truth: Studies of chess players show that years of practice, good coaching and studying games matter enormously. Many of India's young Grandmasters came through chess clubs, school tournaments and online training.

Try this

Set up a board and play a game where you write down every move, like professionals do. Then replay it from your notes. Can you find the one move where the game turned? Grandmasters study their own games this way.
