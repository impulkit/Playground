What does it take to become a chess Grandmaster?

In 1988 India had one chess Grandmaster. On Diwali day 2026, it got its hundredth.

The short answer

A Grandmaster is the highest title in chess, apart from World Champion. It is given for life by FIDE, the world chess federation.

India now has 100 of them. Thirty-eight years ago it had exactly one. More chess clubs, coaches, online training and a famous role model have turned India into one of the strongest chess countries on Earth.

Surprising but true

India's 96th, 97th, 98th, 99th and 100th Grandmasters all earned their titles in just five months, between July and November 2026.

Why it's in the news

On Sunday, 8 November 2026, 22-year-old Anuj Shrivatri from Madhya Pradesh won the 7th ASEAN Plus Individual Open Chess Championship in the Philippines. He lost his very first game, then fought back to win the tournament. The victory completed his requirements for the Grandmaster title, making him India's 100th and the first ever from his state.

He is the fifth Indian to become a Grandmaster since July 2026.

How do you earn the title?

FIDE gives every player a rating, a number that goes up when you beat strong players and down when you lose. Beginners might be rated around 1000. To become a Grandmaster you usually need a rating of 2500, and three "norms". A norm is an outstanding result in a tournament against many strong players, including players from other countries. Winning certain big championships can also count.

FIDE first gave out the title in 1950, to 27 players. Today there are about 2,000 Grandmasters in the whole world.

From one to a hundred

Chess itself grew from chaturanga, an Indian game played more than 1,400 years ago. Yet modern India waited a long time for its first Grandmaster. Viswanathan Anand earned the title in 1988, and went on to become World Champion five times. Children across the country saw that an Indian could reach the top.

Since then, chess academies have opened in many cities, especially in Tamil Nadu. Online games let a child in a small town play strong opponents every day, and computers that use a clever algorithm to check every move help players learn from mistakes. Young Indians such as Gukesh D, who became World Champion in 2024, are now among the best players in the world.

What happens next

FIDE formally confirms new titles at its regular council meetings. Meanwhile, many more young Indian players are close to earning their norms, so the count of 100 will not stay put for long.

Myth or fact?

Myth: You have to be a genius born with a special brain to become a Grandmaster.

Truth: Studies of chess players show that years of practice, good coaching and studying games matter enormously. Many of India's young Grandmasters came through chess clubs, school tournaments and online training.

Try this

Set up a board and play a game where you write down every move, like professionals do. Then replay it from your notes. Can you find the one move where the game turned? Grandmasters study their own games this way.
