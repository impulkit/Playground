What is inside an egg?

An eggshell looks solid, but it has thousands of tiny holes, so a growing chick can breathe through it.

The short answer

An egg is a package designed to grow a chick. On the outside is the shell. Just inside are two thin skins, called membranes. At the wide end is a small air pocket. Then comes the egg white, which is mostly water and protein, and in the middle, the yellow yolk, which is packed with fat, protein and vitamins.

Look closely at a raw egg and you'll see twisted white cords on either side of the yolk. These are the chalazae, which hold the yolk in the middle like a hammock. Most eggs sold in shops come from hens that never met a rooster, so they are not fertilised and will never become chicks.

Surprising but true

The colour of a yolk depends mostly on what the hen eats. Hens fed lots of maize or marigold petals lay eggs with deep orange yolks.

A shell that breathes

An eggshell is made of calcium carbonate, the same stuff as chalk and limestone. A hen needs a lot of calcium to make one, which is why farmers often give hens crushed shells or grit.

The shell is dotted with thousands of tiny pores, too small to see. In a fertilised egg, oxygen comes in and carbon dioxide goes out through them as the chick grows. A thin coating on the outside, called the bloom, helps keep germs out. In the USA, eggs are washed before sale, which removes the bloom, so they must be kept in the fridge.

What the white and yolk do

The yolk is the chick's food store. It holds fat, protein, iron and vitamins. On its surface is a tiny pale spot called the germinal disc. In a fertilised egg, that's where the chick starts to form.

The white cushions the yolk and holds extra water and protein. It also contains natural substances that fight bacteria. When you crack an egg, you can see that the white comes in two parts: a thick layer around the yolk and a runnier layer outside.

The cooking trick

Raw egg white is clear because its proteins are curled up into tight balls. Heat makes them unravel and tangle into a firm white mesh, which is why the white turns solid and opaque.

That's also why eggs are so useful in baking. They hold cakes together, trap air when whisked, and help batters set in the oven. Whisk an egg white for a few minutes and it can swell to several times its size, into white foam called meringue.

Big eggs and small eggs

A hen's egg is small compared with an ostrich egg, which can weigh about 1.4 kg, as much as about two dozen hen's eggs. The tiniest bird eggs, laid by some hummingbirds, are smaller than a jelly bean.

Before it is fertilised, an ostrich yolk is part of a single giant egg cell, among the biggest cells of any living animal. Bird eggs come in many colours, from the blue eggs of robins to the speckled eggs of plovers.

India and the egg

India is one of the world's biggest egg producers, with Andhra Pradesh, Tamil Nadu and Telangana leading. Namakkal in Tamil Nadu is so full of poultry farms that it is often called India's egg city.

Many Indian states serve boiled eggs in school mid-day meals because they are cheap and full of protein. Other states don't, out of respect for families who are vegetarian. Egg bhurji, the spiced scrambled egg cooked on street griddles, is a late-night favourite in many cities.

Myth or fact?

Myth: Brown eggs are healthier than white eggs.

Truth: Shell colour depends on the breed of the hen. Brown and white eggs have the same nutrition. What the hen eats and how fresh the egg is matter far more.

Try this

Fill a bowl with water and gently lower in an egg from a new box and, if you can, one that is a few weeks old. Watch which lies flat, which stands on its end, and which floats. Ask an adult before cracking any egg that floats.
