How were the pyramids of Giza built?

About 2.3 million stone blocks, stacked 4,500 years ago, without engines or cranes.

The short answer

The Great Pyramid was built about 4,500 years ago for the pharaoh Khufu, as his tomb. Workers cut limestone blocks, dragged them on wooden sledges, and pulled them up ramps.

Archaeologists found the builders' town nearby, with bakeries and tombs. They were organised teams of Egyptian workers, not enslaved people, as old stories said. Estimates of how many people worked on it at a time range from about 10,000 to 30,000, and that it took about 20 years.

The Great Pyramid is the oldest of the Seven Wonders of the Ancient World, and the only one still standing.

Surprising but true

Cleopatra lived closer in time to the Moon landing than to the building of the Great Pyramid.

Three pyramids and a Sphinx

Giza, on the edge of modern Cairo beside the Nile, has three big pyramids. The largest is Khufu's. The second belongs to his son Khafre, and the smallest to Menkaure. Nearby crouches the Great Sphinx, a lion with a human head, carved from the bedrock.

Pharaohs were buried with treasure and supplies for the afterlife, and their bodies were preserved as mummies. Robbers broke into the pyramids long ago, so most of the treasure is gone.

The wet-sand trick

An ancient Egyptian painting shows a worker pouring water in front of a sledge carrying a statue. In 2014, scientists showed why: wet sand is much firmer, so a sledge needs about half the pulling force. The right amount of water cuts the friction between the sledge and the ground.

Workers also used levers to lift and nudge blocks into place, and copper chisels and hard stone pounders to shape them. An average block weighs about 2.5 tonnes, as much as two small cars. The granite slabs above Khufu's burial chamber are far heavier, and came from Aswan, about 800 km up the Nile.

How were mummies made?Egyptian embalmers pulled out the brain through the nose with a hook, but left the heart exactly where it was.

The diary of Merer

In 2013, archaeologists at Wadi al-Jarf, a port on the Red Sea, found the oldest inscribed papyrus scrolls ever discovered. Some were the logbook of an official named Merer.

Merer led a team of about 40 men. His notes describe ferrying fine white limestone by boat along the Nile and canals, from quarries at Tura to Giza, for Khufu's pyramid. It is like reading a building site diary from 4,500 years ago.

How tall?

The Great Pyramid was about 146 m tall, the tallest building made by people for about 3,800 years, until a cathedral spire in England passed it in the 1300s. Its stones were once covered in smooth, shining white limestone. Most of that casing was later taken for other buildings, and the top is missing, so today it stands about 138 m.

Its sides line up almost exactly with north, south, east and west. Egyptians probably used the stars to find true north.

The ramp puzzle

Nobody knows exactly how the highest blocks were raised. Some experts suggest one long straight ramp. Others think a ramp zigzagged up one face or wrapped around the pyramid. One architect has even argued for a ramp hidden inside. In 2018, a steep ramp with post holes for ropes was found at an ancient quarry, showing one method Egyptians used.

Meanwhile in India

Around the same time, the great cities of the Indus Valley had straight streets and covered drains. And far to the north, woolly mammoths still lived on a remote Arctic island, where the last of them survived until about 4,000 years ago.

Myth or fact?

Myth: The pyramids are so precise that ancient people couldn't have built them without help from aliens or lost technology.

Truth: Archaeologists have found the quarries, tools, ramps, workers' town and even a logbook from the building project. The pyramids show what skilled, well-organised people can do with copper tools, ropes, sledges and a great deal of time.

Try this

Build a pyramid from sugar cubes, dice or building blocks. Make the bottom layer 4 by 4, then 3 by 3, then 2 by 2, then 1 on top. Count the blocks you used (it should be 30). Now work out how many a 5-layer pyramid needs. The Great Pyramid has around 200 layers.
