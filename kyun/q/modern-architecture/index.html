What makes a building modern?

In the 1900s architects threw away decorations, columns and carved stone, and built with steel, concrete and glass instead. Many people hated it at first.

The short answer

Modern architecture is a style that grew in the early 1900s. Its architects wanted buildings to be honest about what they were made of and what they were for. They used new materials like steel, reinforced concrete and large sheets of glass, and they removed most decoration.

A modern building usually has flat roofs, clean lines, open spaces inside and big windows. Its shape comes from its use. An American architect, Louis Sullivan, summed this up in 1896 with a phrase that became famous: "form follows function".

Surprising but true

Chandigarh, one of India's first planned cities after independence, was designed largely by the Swiss-French architect Le Corbusier, who often compared its plan to a human body, with a head, a heart and lungs.

New materials, new shapes

For thousands of years big buildings stood up on thick walls of stone or brick. The walls carried all the weight, so the taller the building, the thicker the walls at the bottom.

In the 1800s, factories began making strong iron and then steel. Engineers learned to build a skeleton frame of steel beams, like the bones of a body, which carried the weight instead. Walls became thin skins that could even be made of glass. Reinforced concrete, concrete with steel rods inside, could be poured into almost any shape. Iron structures like the Eiffel Tower showed how strong and light a frame could be.

Going up

After a great fire in 1871, Chicago rebuilt quickly with tall office buildings on steel frames. The Home Insurance Building there, finished in 1885, is often called the first skyscraper.

Tall buildings only worked because of another invention, the safety elevator. Without it, nobody would rent an office on the 20th floor. Soon cities like Chicago and New York were racing upwards.

The Bauhaus and the glass box

In 1919 the architect Walter Gropius founded a school in Germany called the Bauhaus. It taught that art, craft and industry should work together, and that good design should be simple, useful and affordable. Its teachers designed buildings, furniture, lamps and even typefaces.

Many Bauhaus teachers later moved to the United States. One, Ludwig Mies van der Rohe, became known for elegant glass and steel towers. He is often linked with the phrase "less is more", which summed up the modern love of simplicity.

Modern India

After independence, India's leaders wanted buildings that looked towards the future. Prime Minister Jawaharlal Nehru invited Le Corbusier to design much of Chandigarh, the new capital of Punjab, in the 1950s. Its concrete government buildings, with bold shapes and deep shadows, are now a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Indian architects then made modern architecture their own. Balkrishna Doshi, who had worked with Le Corbusier, designed low-cost housing and campuses that suit India's heat and monsoon. In 2018 he became the first Indian to win the Pritzker Prize, architecture's top award. Laurie Baker, a British-born architect who settled in Kerala, built cheap, cool homes from local brick with clever gaps that let in air and light.

Loved and hated

Modern architecture changed cities everywhere. Its supporters say it gave people cleaner, brighter homes and offices, and it could be built fast for growing cities. Its critics say some modern buildings feel cold and grey, and that rows of identical concrete blocks made neighbourhoods dull.

Later architects mixed modern ideas with colour, curves and local traditions. The Lotus Temple in Delhi, opened in 1986, uses concrete covered in white marble to form 27 petals. Like many of the best designers of everyday things, its architects began with a simple question: what is this building for?

Myth or fact?

Myth: Modern architecture means any building that is new.

Truth: Modern architecture is a particular style and set of ideas from roughly the 1900s to the 1970s. A building put up last year might copy an old style, while a modernist house can be nearly a hundred years old.
