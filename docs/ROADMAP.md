# Roadmap

## Before launch
- Expert review of all 146 stories, plus a US curriculum pass (NGSS-aligned science, US history/civics stories alongside Indian ones)
- Hand-pick and cache a hero photo per story (today they resolve live from Wikipedia lead images), and add 2–4 gallery photos per story
- COPPA (US) and DPDP Act (India) review. No accounts; keep data on the device
- Native iOS and Android wrappers, with in-app purchase through the App Store and Play billing, behind a parent gate

## Growth
- 500 stories by launch, then a new set every week (the Today feed depends on it)
- Hindi and Spanish editions
- US content: national parks, the space race, US dinosaurs (T. rex in Montana, Hell Creek)
- Teacher mode: share a set of stories with a class

## Kyun Plus (US)
- Free: every story, 1 quiz a day
- Plus: unlimited quizzes, subject quizzes, read-aloud, offline packs, family sharing
- Test prices before launch (placeholders: $3.99/month, $29.99/year)
