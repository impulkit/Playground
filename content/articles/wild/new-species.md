## The short answer

Scientists compare the animal very carefully with every similar species already known. They look at its body, bones, colours, sounds and, today, its [DNA](?dna).

If it is clearly different, and the difference has built up over a long time, they publish a scientific description and give it a new two-part name. Only then is it officially a new [species](?species).

## Hidden in plain sight

Some new species are not hiding in unexplored jungles. They have been seen many times, but everyone thought they were the same as a known animal.

That was true of two howler monkeys named in 2026. Researchers read the genomes, the complete DNA instructions, of 11 kinds of howler monkey and matched them with measurements of old skulls and skins in museums. Animals that had been lumped together as one species turned out to belong to separate family lines.

## Rivers that make new species

The [Amazon](brazil-amazon) is crossed by giant rivers. Howler monkeys spend almost their whole lives high in the forest canopy. To them, a wide river is like a wall.

When a group of animals is cut off for a very long time, it slowly changes in its own way. Rivers have kept howler populations apart for millions of years, helping new species form. This is one reason [rainforests](rainforests) are bursting with so many kinds of life. India's [Western Ghats](western-ghats), full of separate hills and valleys, are another hotspot where new frogs, insects and plants are found every year.

## Who are the howlers?

Howler monkeys live in Central and South America. They eat mostly leaves and use their strong tail like a fifth hand. At dawn, males roar to tell other groups where they are. A hollow bone in the throat makes the sound boom for several kilometres, which puts howlers among the [loudest animals](extreme-loudest-animals) on land.

Naming a species matters. Once it has a name, it can be counted, studied and protected. Scientists warn that the new howlers may already be at risk, because their forest [habitat](?habitat) is being cleared for farms.
