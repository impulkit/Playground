## The short answer

Yes, the seas of the dinosaur age had real monsters. But they weren't dinosaurs. Dinosaurs lived on land. The giants of the sea were other kinds of [reptiles](?reptile) that had moved back into the water.

There were three main groups. Ichthyosaurs looked a lot like dolphins. [Plesiosaurs](marine-reptiles-plesiosaur) had four big flippers, and many had very [long necks](sauropod-necks). Mosasaurs were huge sea lizards with snapping jaws. Alongside them swam giant turtles and early [sharks](sharks-old).

Like whales today, all of them breathed air. They had to come up to the surface to fill their lungs.

## Fish-lizards with dinner-plate eyes

The name ichthyosaur means "fish lizard". They had pointed snouts, a fin on the back and a tail shaped like a crescent. Yet they evolved from four-legged reptiles that lived on land. This shows how the same problem, moving fast through water, can lead very different animals to the same shape. Scientists call it convergent evolution.

Some ichthyosaurs had eyes as wide as a dinner plate, probably for hunting in dim, deep water. They didn't lay eggs on beaches. In Germany, fossils have been found of mothers that died while giving birth, with babies coming out tail first, just as baby dolphins do.

## Long necks and four flippers

Plesiosaurs swam by beating four flippers, a bit like sea turtles or [penguins](penguin-flight) "flying" underwater. One kind, Elasmosaurus, had a neck with over 70 bones. A swan has about 25, and a giraffe has only 7, the same as you. Nobody is quite sure how plesiosaurs used such necks, but they may have helped them sneak up on shoals of fish without being noticed.

The first nearly complete plesiosaur skeleton was found in 1823 at Lyme Regis in England by [Mary Anning](mary-anning). Years earlier, she and her brother had also dug up one of the first ichthyosaur skulls ever found. She had little schooling, but she became one of the most skilled fossil hunters of her time.

## Lizards that ruled the waves

Mosasaurs came last and grew largest, with some reaching about 15 m, longer than a city bus. They had powerful tails, flippers and a second row of teeth on the roof of the mouth to grip slippery prey.

Their fossils turn up in surprising places. One of the first was dug out of a quarry near Maastricht in the Netherlands in the 1700s. In Kansas, in the middle of the USA, the rocks are full of mosasaurs and plesiosaurs. That is because a shallow sea once split North America in two.

India has its own sea monster too. In 2016, scientists found an ichthyosaur skeleton about 150 million years old near the village of Lodai in Kutch, Gujarat. It was the first one from that age found in India.

## What happened to them

Ichthyosaurs died out about 90 million years ago, long before the dinosaurs. Plesiosaurs and mosasaurs lasted until 66 million years ago. Then [the asteroid](extinction) that ended the age of dinosaurs wiped them out too.

Some sea reptiles did make it through, as you can read in [who survived](who-survived). Sea turtles and crocodiles are still with us. A 4 m sea turtle called Archelon, as long as a car, swam in the same seas as the mosasaurs. Its smaller relatives still come ashore on Indian beaches to lay their eggs, like the [olive ridleys](sea-turtles) of Odisha.
