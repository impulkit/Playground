## The short answer

Armed forces are a country's military: its army on land, navy at sea and air force in the sky. Their main job is to defend the country from attack and protect its borders, coasts and airspace.

But that is only part of what they do. They rescue people from floods and earthquakes, carry supplies to cut-off areas, evacuate citizens from war zones abroad and serve as United Nations peacekeepers. In most democracies, including India and the USA, the armed forces take orders from the elected government, not the other way round. This is called civilian control.

## India's three services

India's Army is one of the largest in the world. It guards long land borders, from the deserts of Rajasthan to the icy heights of Ladakh and the forests of the north-east. The Navy patrols the Indian Ocean, and the Air Force flies fighter jets, transport planes and [helicopters](helicopters). A separate Coast Guard watches India's coastline.

In 2019, the government created the post of Chief of Defence Staff, to help the three services plan and work together. Army Day, on 15 January, marks the day in 1949 when K. M. Cariappa became the first Indian commander-in-chief of the Indian Army.

The forces have changed in other ways too. In 2016, the Air Force commissioned its first three women [fighter pilots](jobs-pilot), and women now serve in many roles once closed to them. Soldiers, sailors and air crew join from every state. Many army regiments still carry old names of regions and communities, such as the Madras Regiment, the Sikh Regiment and the Gorkha Rifles.

## Rescue and relief

When floods struck [Uttarakhand](uttarakhand) in June 2013, roads were washed away and thousands of pilgrims and villagers were stranded. The Army built temporary bridges and the Air Force flew helicopter missions to lift people out. In 2018, during the [Kerala floods](floods-why), military helicopters plucked people off rooftops, and Navy boats reached flooded villages.

The forces also help abroad. After a huge earthquake in Turkey and Syria in 2023, India sent [army doctors](people-army-life) and rescue teams. The US military often carries aid to disaster zones around the world, using its large fleet of ships and transport planes.

## America's six services

The USA has six military services: the Army, Navy, Air Force, Marine Corps, Coast Guard and Space Force. The Space Force, set up in December 2019, looks after military satellites. The US military has bases in many countries and spends more than any other in the world.

The US President is commander in chief, but only Congress can formally declare war. This split was deliberate. The writers of the Constitution did not want one person to be able to take the country into war alone.

## A country without an army

In 1948, after a short civil war, Costa Rica in Central America abolished its army. Its leader, José Figueres, wanted the money spent on education and health instead. Today the country has police but no military, and it is known for its schools, health care and protected forests.

Most countries still keep armed forces, and [world wars](world-war-two) in the last century showed what happens when they are used on a huge scale. That is one reason the [United Nations](united-nations) was created, to settle disputes before they turn into fighting. Some people argue that strong armies prevent wars by making attack too risky, while others think spending on weapons makes conflict more likely. Countries have argued about that balance for centuries.
